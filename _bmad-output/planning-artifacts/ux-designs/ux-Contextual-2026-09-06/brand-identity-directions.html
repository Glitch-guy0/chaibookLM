03 — Usage Rules

Do's and Don'ts

Rules that keep the identity consistent across products, marketing, and social.

Clear Space & Sizing

Do
Maintain minimum clear space equal to the symbol height on all sides. Minimum symbol size: 24px digital, 12mm print.
Don't
Never place the logo inside busy photographic backgrounds or overlapping UI elements without sufficient contrast backing.

Color Application

Do
Use the yellow accent mark only on the primary CTA or the brand symbol highlight. One accent per viewport.
Don't
Never apply the citation cyan to buttons, badges, or navigation. Cyan is reserved for source-linked elements only.

Typography Pairing

Do
Use Space Mono for all headings, labels, and badges. Use Inter for body paragraphs and chat responses. Never mix a third typeface.
Don't
Never use decorative, script, or serif typefaces. The identity is utilitarian and monospaced — ornamental fonts break the voice.

Shadow & Border System

Do
All interactive elements use the standard elevation flow: 4px → hover 6px → pressed 0px. Zero blur, solid ink.
Don't
Never use soft/diffuse shadows, gradients, or rounded corners beyond the pill badge (9999px). No border-radius above 4px.

04 — Generation Prompts

Image Generation Prompts

Copy-paste prompts for generating each logo route as a high-res asset. Optimized for flux-pro-ultra and gpt-image-2.

Route A — Document Mark (Symbol Only)

Minimal geometric logo symbol on a warm off-white background (#F4F4F0). A perfect square with 3px black (#111111) stroke, sharp corners, no border-radius. Inside the square: a horizontal yellow (#FFE500) bar at the vertical center, 60% width, 3px tall, aligned left with padding. Below the bar, two faint horizontal lines in light gray suggesting text. Style: neo-brutalist, hard edges, zero blur, flat colors only. No gradients, no shadows, no 3D effects. Centered composition, generous whitespace around the symbol. Aspect ratio 1:1, high resolution.

Route A — Full Lockup

Horizontal brand lockup on warm off-white (#F4F4F0). Left: a 48px square symbol with 2px black stroke, containing a yellow horizontal bar at center. Right: the word "CONTEXTUAL" in Space Mono Bold, 18px, black (#111111), letter-spacing 0.02em. Alignment: vertically centered. No tagline. Clean, minimal, neo-brutalist aesthetic. Flat colors, no gradients, no shadows. Aspect ratio 3:1, high resolution.

Route B — Citation Bracket (Symbol Only)

Minimal geometric logo symbol on warm off-white (#F4F4F0). Two heavy angular brackets facing inward, constructed from 4px black (#111111) strokes with square line-caps. Between the brackets: a horizontal cyan (#00E5FF) rectangle, centered, representing a highlighted passage. The brackets are taller than the bar, creating vertical emphasis. Style: neo-brutalist, sharp geometry, flat colors, zero blur. No gradients, no rounded corners. Centered composition. Aspect ratio 1:1, high resolution.

Route B — Full Lockup

Horizontal brand lockup on warm off-white (#F4F4F0). Left: a symbol of two inward-facing black brackets with a cyan horizontal bar between them. Right: the word "CONTEXTUAL" in Space Mono Bold, 18px, black (#111111). Vertically centered alignment. Flat neo-brutalist style, no gradients, no shadows. Aspect ratio 3:1, high resolution.

Route C — Monospace C (Symbol Only)

Minimal geometric logo symbol on warm off-white (#F4F4F0). A bold, geometric letter "C" constructed with a 5px black (#111111) stroke, square line-caps, open on the right side. At the terminal of the C (the open end), a small yellow (#FFE500) horizontal rectangle fills the gap, acting as an accent notch. The C is built on a grid, with sharp angles matching a monospace typeface. Style: neo-brutalist, flat, zero blur, no gradients. Centered composition. Aspect ratio 1:1, high resolution.

Route C — Full Lockup

Horizontal brand lockup on warm off-white (#F4F4F0). Left: a geometric letter "C" symbol with black stroke and yellow terminal accent bar. Right: the word "CONTEXTUAL" in Space Mono Bold, 18px, black (#111111). Vertically centered. Flat neo-brutalist style. Aspect ratio 3:1, high resolution.
