How does Raft handle partition recovery?

When the partition heals, the minority leader steps down and reverts to follower. It discards any uncommitted entries from its term and adopts the new leader's log via the appending entries catch-up phase [1].

The committed-index is the safety anchor — a leader never overwrites already-committed entries [2].
