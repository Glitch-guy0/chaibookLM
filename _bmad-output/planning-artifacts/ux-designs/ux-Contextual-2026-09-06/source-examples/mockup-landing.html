How does Raft handle partition recovery?
Raft restores availability once a partitioned leader regains a heartbeat quorum, then truncates and overwrites entries from the new leader's log [1].
In MIT's lecture, this is the "commit index" catch-up phase [2].
ORIGINAL VIEWPage 14 / 28

4.2 Leader Election

When a follower stops receiving heartbeats from the leader, it increments its current term and transitions to candidate state. The candidate requests votes from a majority of servers and becomes leader if it receives enough votes.

4.3 Partition Recovery — When the partition heals, the stale leader reverts to follower status upon discovering a higher term. It discards any uncommitted entries that were not replicated to a majority during the partition window.

The commit index advances only after the new leader has replicated entries to a quorum. Clients that received responses from the stale leader during the partition may need to retry, as those commits were not durable.
