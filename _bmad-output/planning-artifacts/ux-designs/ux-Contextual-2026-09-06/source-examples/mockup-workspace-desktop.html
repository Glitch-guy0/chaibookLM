How does Raft handle a leader crash during replication?

When a leader crashes, followers detect the missed heartbeat and start a new election with randomized timeouts. The candidate with the most up-to-date log wins, then replicates a new no-op or client entry to commit the previous term's state [1].

In the MIT lecture, this is framed around the safety property — a leader never overwrites entries from an already-committed log [2].

Section 5.4 — Leadership changes

Raft also handles the case where a server is removed from the configuration. If the removed leader continues to receive and replicate entries, those entries will never be committed because the removed leader is not part of the majority. The cluster can make progress only after the removed leader steps down.

During a partition, the minority leader can be deposed. Once the partitions heal, the deposed leader reverts to follower and discards any uncommitted entries from its term, then adopts the new leader's log.
